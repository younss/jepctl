Why this decision

                                    
Frame-by-frame breakdown of the match. The same fields come back from /api/gestures/match.

                                

                                

                                    method: --
                                    
                                

                            


                            

                                How the scores are computed
                                
Raw cosine: direct similarity between the frame and the prototype. With the same background, face and lighting it saturates near 1.0 for every gesture and discriminates nothing.

                                
Contrastive: the centroid of all prototypes (the shared part: background, torso, light) is removed, then what remains is compared. That is the gesture's own signature.

                                
Combined = 0.35 × raw + 0.65 × contrastive. Detected when combined ≥ threshold and the lead over the runner-up ≥ margin. A winning Neutral slot blocks detection.
